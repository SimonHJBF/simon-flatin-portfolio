Analytics

If you allow it, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics sets cookies and sends information about the visit to Google. Nothing is stored or sent unless you choose Allow, and you can change your choice at any time.

Links in applications

When I send this site with a job application, the link can carry a short code naming the application, for example the office I applied to. If you allow analytics, the code tells me that the link was opened. It is not tied to your name, and I use it for nothing else.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered by Netlify, which hosts the site. I keep them only as long as the conversation needs them.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete what I hold about you. You can also complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.
