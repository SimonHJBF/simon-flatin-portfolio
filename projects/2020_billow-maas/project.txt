Billow by the Maas is a waterfront design proposal for a site along the Maas river in Rotterdam. The project responds to the industrial heritage of the port city while proposing a new urban typology that flows with the river — undulating, layered and in constant dialogue with water.

Developed as part of the ON6 design studio at TU Delft, the work challenged designing at the scale of the city block. Billow proposes a mixed-use programme woven into a structural form that mirrors the rhythmic motion of the river: rippled facades, cascading terraces and a public waterfront promenade that connects the project to the surrounding city.

The proposal investigates how contemporary waterfront development can acknowledge Rotterdam's history as a port city — not by mimicking the past, but by channelling the energy and logic of water into architectural form.
