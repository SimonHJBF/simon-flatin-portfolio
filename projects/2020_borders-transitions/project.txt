Borders and Transitions is a photography series exploring the moments where one spatial condition gives way to another — where land meets water, where cultivated ground returns to wild, where human intervention dissolves into landscape. Shot during a summer journey through Norway, the series documents these thresholds with an architectural eye.

Each image is a study in edge conditions: the frame, the horizon line, the boundary between inside and outside. The series reflects a recurring preoccupation in my practice — the idea that the most interesting architecture happens not at the centre of a space, but at its edges, where different systems negotiate a shared boundary.

The work was developed in parallel with research into Norwegian building traditions and their relationship to the landscape, and has since informed both design projects and the visual grammar of my photographic practice.
