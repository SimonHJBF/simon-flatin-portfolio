The Omsorgsgruppen Våler project is an assisted living and care facility commission in the municipality of Våler, Innlandet. The design addresses the growing demand for dignified residential care environments in rural Norway — spaces that prioritise daylight, connection to landscape and a domestic scale that resists institutional character.

The proposal organises the programme around a series of clustered residential wings, each of twelve units, connected by a shared common spine. This spine — containing communal living areas, a kitchen and an activity room — is oriented south toward the agricultural landscape, with generous glazing that brings the changing of the seasons directly into the shared life of the building.

Material choices respond to the region's timber building tradition: cross-laminated timber structure, façades in vertical untreated pine, and interiors that use natural materials throughout. The project is currently in the design development phase.
