SESC Mogi das Cruzes is a competition entry for a new cultural and leisure centre in the municipality of Mogi das Cruzes, east of São Paulo. The SESC (Serviço Social do Comércio) network has a long tradition of commissioning architecturally ambitious public buildings — most famously Lina Bo Bardi's SESC Pompéia — and this proposal continues in that spirit.

The design proposes a building that is simultaneously civic infrastructure and landscape. A series of terraced platforms descends toward a public park, housing a theatre, workshops, a library, sports facilities and a public swimming pool. The platforms are planted with native Atlantic Forest species, so that the building is gradually claimed by the landscape over time.

The project reflects my interest in architecture that serves as common ground — spaces that belong to everyone, structured by topography rather than walls, and that age into their setting with increasing grace.
