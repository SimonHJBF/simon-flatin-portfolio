Stilt House is a design proposal for a dwelling in an extreme flood environment, developed as part of the Extreme studio at TU Delft. The brief asked students to design for conditions at the edge of habitability — in this case, a site periodically submerged by rising water levels.

The proposal elevates the living programme high above the flood plain on a structural stilts system, freeing the ground level for water to pass through unimpeded. The dwelling is organised vertically rather than horizontally: a tight core of services at the centre, with living spaces arranged around it at different heights, each oriented to a specific view or quality of light.

The project investigates how constraints imposed by an extreme environment — structural, programmatic, material — can become generative forces rather than mere problems to solve. The result is a dwelling that is, paradoxically, most at home when surrounded by water.
