Crafting Places that Echo Mountains,
Rivers & the Spaces Between

My practice is a dialogue between two worlds: the crisp Nordic light that shaped my childhood and the layered landscapes where I now design. I read terrain first — through drone photogrammetry, hand sketches or a block of clay — then guide projects from concept through construction with the same curiosity. The goal: architecture that matures gracefully, treads lightly and feels inevitable in its setting.

Services

Full-service design from site analysis and concept through planning, detailing and construction oversight. I work across scales — from a single room to an urban quarter — always grounding proposals in the specific character of place, light and material.

Revit-based BIM coordination for complex multi-disciplinary projects, including clash detection, information management and digital twin library development for ongoing facility management.

Precise technical drawing packages — plans, sections, elevations and details — for planning applications, building permits and contractor tendering. Fluent in AutoCAD, Revit and Vectorworks.

Exhibition-quality architectural models at any scale, from study maquettes to museum commission pieces. My models for TU Delft have been exhibited at the Venice Architecture Biennale 2023. Materials range from laser-cut plywood and card to hand-carved stone and resin.

Aerial photogrammetry surveys producing high-resolution point clouds and 3D meshes of sites, landscapes and existing buildings. Useful for planning, heritage recording, contractor briefings and spatial research.

Focused research into material behaviour, tectonic systems, and formal strategies. Suitable for academic institutions, design competitions, and practices wanting rigorous conceptual groundwork before committing to a design direction.

Preparation and management of planning submissions in Norway and the Netherlands, including neighbourhood consultation documents, heritage impact assessments, and responses to authority queries.

Integrating circular material flows, passive systems and low-carbon strategies into projects from the outset. I draw on both Nordic and international precedent to find solutions that reduce embodied energy without sacrificing quality or character.

I work regularly across Norway, the Netherlands, Poland and Brazil. All services are available remotely via structured video consultations, shared cloud models and collaborative drawing reviews.
