Architecture, drawings
and models

Scroll

I am an architect working between Norway and São Paulo, mostly online, with clients in Norway, Sweden, Portugal, the United States and Brazil. The work runs from planning applications and competition proposals to visualisation, physical models and the software I build for the building industry. Most projects still start with a physical model on the desk.

Services

01 Architecture, from sketch to building

Design from site analysis and concept through to planning, detailing and construction follow-up, at scales from a single room to a masterplan. Recent examples are the Bjørnåsen house addition in Larkollen, the SESC Mogi das Cruzes competition proposal in Brazil and the Arctic Symphony graduation project in Alta.

02 Building applications in Norway

Preparation and follow-up of building applications in Norway, including the supporting documentation and answers to the municipality's questions. Most recently a change-of-use application for a care building for Omsorgsgruppen in Våler.

03 Drawings and BIM models

Technical drawing packages, plans, sections, elevations and details, for building applications and contractor tendering. Revit models with coordination and information management, and parametric workflows in Rhino and Grasshopper where geometry is better driven by rules than drawn by hand. Revit, Rhino, Grasshopper and AutoCAD at an advanced level.

04 Architectural models

Exhibition-quality architectural models at any scale, from study maquettes to commissioned pieces. My models for TU Delft were exhibited at the Venice Architecture Biennale 2023 at Palazzo Mora and at the faculty in Delft.

05 Drone survey of sites and buildings

Aerial photogrammetry surveys producing point clouds and 3D meshes of sites, landscapes and existing buildings. Useful for planning, site recording, contractor briefings and spatial research.

06 Reuse and low-carbon design

Circular material flows, passive systems and low-carbon strategies built into a project from the outset. The Harbour Box proposal, which shared first place in the Upcycle Mall Rotterdam competition, was made from waste materials taken off the Rotterdam docks.

07 Research and material studies

Focused research into material behaviour, tectonic systems and formal strategies. The Arctic Symphony research included a series of sand erosion studies testing how built interventions change sedimentation in a river system.

How a commission starts

  1. Write to me with the address or property number, what you want to build or change, and roughly when.
  2. I reply within two business days, and we talk it through on site or by video.
  3. You get a written offer for the first stage, with a fixed price or an hourly rate, plus VAT. Nothing starts before you have agreed to it.
